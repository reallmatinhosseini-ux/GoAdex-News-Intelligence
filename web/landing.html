before the number.

  3. 03

    T+60 · scored, publicly

    After the release every forecast is scored at 1, 5, 15, 30 and 60 minutes — first move, final move and reversal tested separately. Wins and losses stay on the record.

Our standard

No invented
numbers. Ever.

Most “signals” show you confidence. GoAdex shows you evidence — and says so when there isn’t enough.

  • Validated before published. A direction appears only after its model beats a coin flip on releases it never saw.

  • No look-ahead. Built only from information published before the release, with official publication lags respected.
  • Real prices, real sources. Live bid/ask quotes and official statistics — never simulated data.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How accurate is GoAdex?

We only report accuracy measured out of sample on real price data, with the uncertainty shown next to it — see the live track record above. We won’t quote a win rate we can’t prove.

Which markets and releases does it cover?

Gold (XAUUSD) and EUR/USD around major US and euro-area releases: FOMC, CPI, NFP, PCE, GDP, PPI, retail sales, FOMC minutes and more.

Why would it say “Insufficient Evidence”?

Because a guess dressed up as a forecast is worse than no forecast. If a model hasn’t passed validation, or the inputs are stale, GoAdex tells you exactly that.

Is this gambling?

No. GoAdex is a data-driven research and forecasting product built by GoAdex Holding. It doesn’t take positions or accept stakes. Forecasts are probabilistic and can be wrong; trading involves risk.
